# Havus name and icon

The Havus source code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). That license covers the code. It doesn't cover the **Havus name** or the **Havus app icon** ([`design/AppIcon.svg`](design/AppIcon.svg) and the images rendered from it), which identify the official app by Debi Kurnia.

Havus asks for Full Disk Access and deletes files, so people need to be able to tell the official app apart from copies. A fake "Havus" could do real damage.

## Fine without asking

- Mentioning Havus by name in articles, reviews, videos, tutorials and talks.
- Using the icon when you link to or write about the official app.
- Sharing unmodified official releases for free, for example through a package manager such as Homebrew Cask.
- Saying that your project is "based on Havus" or "a fork of Havus".

## Needs its own name and icon

- A modified version of Havus (a fork) that you share with others.
- Selling Havus, or an app based on it, under the Havus name or with the Havus icon.
- Names and icons that are easy to confuse with Havus, such as "Havus Pro" or "Havus Cleaner" for an app that isn't the official one.

Not sure whether your use is fine? Open an [issue](https://github.com/XNyetok/Havus/issues) and ask.

## Bahasa Indonesia

Kode Havus berlisensi MIT, tapi nama **Havus** dan **ikonnya** nggak termasuk. Kamu boleh nyebut atau ngulas Havus, pakai ikonnya waktu nautin ke app resminya, dan bilang proyekmu "berbasis Havus". Tapi kalau kamu nyebarin versi modifikasi (fork) atau menjualnya, pakai nama dan ikon sendiri, biar orang nggak ketuker sama Havus resmi.
